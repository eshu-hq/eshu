## Decisions
1. Range unit (amends arbiter-11117 item 5 and arbiter-11117e decision 6). A range is a contiguous interval of the keyed index order (package_id, repository_id, subject_digest, c0, advisory_id), cut only at identifier edges. "Whole package_id values" is withdrawn (axios alone is 1,575,325 facts vs a 15,000 budget). A canonical key never spans two ranges; an identifier larger than the budget runs alone and whole. First range unbounded below; last range open-ended on both fact and stored sides. Budget 10,000 facts default, configurable; the 25 s timeout stays a guard; a timed-out range retries at half budget down to one identifier. Guards, fail closed with a deferral reason: (a) each range statement reports whether any of its facts has '|' in an identifier field, at no extra read; (b) a NULL probe on package_id, repository_id and subject_digest runs at sweep start AND at sweep end, and a sweep counts as complete only if both are zero (a row comparison silently drops a row whose middle field is NULL). Operator facts load whole.
2. #11119's cost bar: unchanged and hard: each transaction at most 60 s; stored winners identical to a single-statement resweep. The "sum of batches at most 1.25x" clause is restated as a per-fact bound on one host and one cache state: ranged cold cost at most 1.25 times the full statement's, which on QA is at most 0.99 ms per fact, or 8,294 s total. Decision rule (measurement M3): mean at or under 0.99 with the shipped body: proceed with it. Over 0.99 but at or under with the detoast-once shape: that shape is required, with a digest differential and its statement added to the contract hash set. Over 0.99 with both: the ranged rebuild waits for the narrow store (N2), and the single statement stays as the known cost already accepted in arbiter-11117 item 8. Not waived on a projection. #11131's sweep is not held by this: its bars are 25 s and 24 h, and the lane's 7 h projection meets them.
3. Progress under about 100 percent keyed duty: the range verify tries the resweep lock first; after two misses on the same range it uses the waiting form pg_advisory_xact_lock with SET LOCAL lock_timeout (Postgres grants a released lock to the queued waiter before any later try-lock). A timeout records lock_busy and retries the range. Not serialization as a fix: the single-flight lock already exists, no worker count or batch size changes, the keyed tick loses at most one verify (under 30 s) per locked range. To keep locked ranges rare, filter candidates against the pending set on the writer without the lock, and take the lock only when a non-pending candidate remains (a pending key is recomputed by the keyed tick anyway). #11120 is not a prerequisite. The lock_timeout value is set from M8's tick maximum, not guessed. Over-cap fallback: it must stop running the single-statement full inline. Starting a ranged rebuild from it is rejected as designed (the rebuild leaves scope rows queued, the next tick admits them, the cap trips again). The branch becomes plain chunked consume, which already converts a scope remainder and always progresses. Proof before merge: a seeded over-cap scope backlog drains to empty with winners equal to the single statement and every transaction at most 60 s.
4. #11120 target and order: L is p95 watermark lag at most 15 minutes at QA inflow, held for 6 hours, with keyed duty at or under 75 percent. Capacity bar: at least 1.3 times D(15 min)/900 s on the inflow measured at acceptance: today about 51 identifiers per second, about 20 ms per identifier or 0.17 ms per candidate fact of stream time, 9.4 times the current 5.4/s. The 5-minute point (15x) is not required. Order: first, L5 as a read-only investigation (M1, M2); in parallel, L3 theory proofs (M6), no code until they pass; L1 and L2 are rejected as standalone PRs (about 1.1x each on cold QA, under the issue's own 1.3x stop line; detoast-once survives only inside decision 2 or L3); L4 is deferred behind L3 (with about 2 spare streams of IOPS it gives at most 3x now). First PRs: #11131 is the sweep, the first production PR of the three, and it owns the range primitive; #11119 follows, reusing it; #11120 has no code PR until M1, M2 and M6 report.
5. L4 prerequisites (recorded so the theory can proceed): stamp as its own LAST statement, not per-partition watermark rows (wake and commit are under 2 percent of the tick, so the serialized section is small and no read-side contract changes; switch to per-partition rows only if the measured stamp-to-commit section exceeds 5 percent of the tick at 3 streams); collapse: one owner, outside partition ticks; admission: oldest N per partition; claim locks: one key per partition plus a global key held shared by partition ticks and exclusive by full, rebuild and verify work; scope rows: one owner that only converts scope rows to fact rows and writes no winners (converted rows carry the scope row's captured_at, LEAST on conflict); boundaries persisted from stored sum(source_count), rebalanced only between sweeps. Proofs owed on live Postgres: per-partition late-commit and horizon tests, a deadlock run with the converter and 3 partitions at overlapping keys, EvalPlanQual recheck on the lock-then-delete, equality with the single-stream result.
